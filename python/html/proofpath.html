ProofPath

Follow every claim along the truth path

ProofPath is Noeti’s verification method. It turns an answer into a trail of claims you can audit, customize, and gate before publish.

  • 01 Atomize Break every output into atomic claims — the unit of verification.
  • 02 Witness Collect independent witness classes: records, documents, timelines, compute nodes.
  • 03 Contradict Actively search for disconfirming evidence. Keep conflict visible.
  • 04 Negative evidence Log what was checked and not found. Absence is part of the record.
  • 05 Replay Source → extract → check → verdict. Fully auditable end to end.
  • 06 Publish gate Block contested claims before export. Customize thresholds per desk.
