Refraction Mesh Architecture: Multimodal Decentralized Inference with Verifiable Claims and Fail-Closed Data Protection

Noeti Compute Lab

Independent Research Group · dev@noeticompute.com · noeticompute.com

Abstract

Foundation models concentrate two coupled risks that existing assistant architectures handle poorly: epistemic risk, in which fluent generation outruns grounded verification, and custody risk, in which multimodal case material is retained inside a monopolistic compute boundary [1]–[5]. Automated fact-checking and retrieval-augmented generation improve coverage, yet typically collapse judgment into a single score produced inside a single trust domain [8]–[10]. We propose the Refraction Mesh Architecture (RMA), a systems architecture in which composite multimodal inputs are refracted into typed atomic claims and calculation tasks, probed by orthogonal witness planes, and admitted to export only through a machine-checkable publish gate. We formalize spectral conflict intensity $\kappa_i$, orthogonality $\Omega$, gate predicates $\Gamma$, and a six-layer data-protection stack with fail-closed semantics. RMA couples ProofPath audit packets, privacy sleeves, dual decentralized/centralized routing with explicit provenance, and Proof-of-Inference cosigning [12], [14], [16].

Index Terms—Decentralized inference, multimodal verification, publish gate, privacy sleeves, Proof-of-Inference, systems security.

Introduction

The rapid deployment of large language and multimodal models has changed drafting practice across journalism, law, compliance, and open-source intelligence [1]–[3]. The scientific and operational problem is no longer merely “can the model answer?” but “under what architectural guarantees may an answer become a publishable artifact, and what happens to the underlying media while that answer is produced?”

Empirical work shows that false claims propagate faster than true ones in online networks [4]. Analyses of large generative systems warn that fluency can detach from communicative grounding [5]. Instruction tuning improves helpfulness [3], yet does not by itself create multi-party epistemic checks, durable negative evidence, or custody boundaries for restricted images and sealed documents.

Automated fact-checking has matured into extract–retrieve–verify pipelines [8], [9]. RAG reduces some hallucinations by coupling parametric memory to external corpora [10]. Multi-agent setups improve intermediate structure [6], [7]. Vision–language models enable cross-modal grounding [20], [21]. Journalism verification handbooks, however, insist on independent corroboration, documentation of what was not found, and human authority over publication [17], [18]—practices most model UIs still omit.

We therefore treat verification and data protection as architectural invariants. The refraction metaphor is operational: a composite statement behaves like white light; RMA splits it into typed spectral lines, measures disagreement across orthogonal instruments, and applies a notch filter before export.

A. Research Questions

RQ1 (Epistemics): How can multimodal claims be certified without collapsing to a single model score?
RQ2 (Decentralization): How can inference remain decentralized-first while offering labeled centralized capacity?
RQ3 (Custody): How can sensitive multimodal data be protected under fail-closed policy rather than best-effort redaction?

B. Contributions

(1) We invent and specify RMA for typed multimodal claim verification. (2) We formalize $\kappa_i$, $\Omega$, and $\Gamma$. (3) We define ProofPath packets with first-class negative evidence and Merkle–Ed25519 sealing [13], [16]. (4) We present a dual fabric and a rigorously layered data-protection stack [14], [15]. (5) We provide an evaluation agenda and a live deployment mapping.

Related Work

A. Fault Tolerance and Ledgers

Byzantine agreement requires quora among unreliable parties [11], [12]. RMA borrows quorum intuition for epistemic artifacts—claims—rather than only replica logs. Merkle commitments and ledgers motivate append-only auditability [13], [19]. Content addressing supports immutable extracts for replay.

B. Privacy and Collaborative Learning

Mix networks unlink senders from messages [14]. Differential privacy formalizes disclosure risk [22]. Secure aggregation and federated learning reduce central visibility into raw updates [15]. RMA adapts unlinkability to inference tasks: the compute node should learn the job, not the requester identity.

C. Fact Checking, RAG, Agents, Multimodality

FEVER formalized large-scale claim verification [8]; surveys chart methods and limits [9]. RAG couples generation to retrieved non-parametrics [10]. Multimodal LLMs fuse vision and language [20], [21]. RMA claims a new control plane for when generative output may leave a high-stakes desk—not a new backbone model.

Threat Model and Design Goals

A minority of compute nodes may be Byzantine [11]. Centralized APIs are untrusted for sole judgment and restricted media [5]. Attacks include prompt injection, Sybil witnesses, adversarial images, OCR spoofing, and caption–pixel inconsistency [21].

G1 Multiplicity — no single plane suffices for publication-bound claims. G2 Durability — checks survive as replayable packets. G3 Custody — restricted media defaults on-node; catalog use is explicit. G4 Fail-closed — missing private capacity refuses the job. G5 Human authority — gates assist editors; they do not replace them [18].

Refraction Mesh Architecture

Let multimodal bundle $B=(Q,R,\mathcal{M})$. Refraction is

\[\mathrm{Refract}(B)\mapsto C=\{c_1,\ldots,c_n\}\]

with typed atoms $\tau(c_i)\in\{\mathsf{text},\mathsf{vision},\mathsf{doc},\mathsf{table},\mathsf{calc},\mathsf{cross}\}$. A witness plane maps $(c_i,\mathcal{S})\mapsto v_{i,k}\in\{\mathsf{support},\mathsf{contest},\mathsf{unknown}\}$.

Spectral conflict intensity:

\[\kappa_i=\frac{t_i}{s_i+t_i+\varepsilon},\qquad \varepsilon>0 \tag{1}\]

ProofPath packet:

\[P=\big(Q,R,\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{handles}},C,\{(W_k,v_{i,k})\},\mathcal{N},G,\delta,\sigma\big) \tag{2}\]

Orthogonality of plane errors $E_k$:

\[\Omega = 1 - \frac{2}{m(m-1)}\sum_{k<\ell}\mathrm{Corr}(E_k,E_\ell) \tag{3}\]

Low $\Omega$ diagnoses collapsed diversity (e.g., all compute planes calling one provider) [5].

Weighted spectral measure and soft gate

Planes need not be equipotent. With weights $w_k>0$, $\sum_k w_k=1$, define $S_i=\sum_{k:v_{i,k}=\mathsf{support}} w_k$, $T_i=\sum_{k:v_{i,k}=\mathsf{contest}} w_k$, $U_i=\sum_{k:v_{i,k}=\mathsf{unknown}} w_k$.

\[ \kappa_i^{(w)}=\frac{T_i}{S_i+T_i+\varepsilon},\qquad \tilde{\kappa}_i=\kappa_i^{(w)}\,(1-\alpha U_i) \tag{1b} \]
\[ \Psi(P)=\exp\!\Big(-\beta\sum_{i=1}^{n}\tilde{\kappa}_i\Big)\cdot\mathbf{1}\!\big[\min_i|\mathcal{W}_i|\ge q_{\min}\big] \tag{1c} \]

Sleeve information bound

\[ I(U;L_m)\le I(U;R)+I(U;M\mid R) \tag{1d} \]

Cross-modal consistency functional

\[ \phi(c)=1-\frac{\|e_a(c)-e_b(c)\|_2}{\|e_a(c)\|_2+\|e_b(c)\|_2+\varepsilon} \tag{1e} \]

The modality plane returns $\mathsf{contest}$ when $\phi(c)<\tau_\phi$. Soft score $\Psi$ recovers hard gate $\Gamma$ by thresholding under the strict contest rule.

Multimodal Calculation Plane

Multimodal desks do not only chat; they calculate. Let $\mathrm{Calc}$ be a deterministic interpreter over numeric relations:

\[\mathrm{Calc}(c_i)\mapsto\{\mathsf{match},\mathsf{mismatch},\mathsf{n/a}\} \tag{4}\]

A $\mathsf{mismatch}$ increments $t_i$ exactly as a contesting witness would. This is deliberate: language models are strong at fluency and weak at reliable arithmetic consistency with tables [5], [1]. A calc veto is cheaper and more auditable than hoping a second model “notices.” Streaming tokens remain a latency surface; sealed packets are the product of record.

Decentralized Dual Fabric

Principle: decentralized-first, centralized-explicit. Roles comprise hub $\mathcal{H}$, sleeve relay $\mathcal{R}$, compute $\mathcal{C}$, witness feeds $\mathcal{F}$, and desk $\mathcal{D}$. Network-finality requires $|\sigma|\ge q$ for quorum $q$ [12], [19]. Images and sealed PDFs are high-value exfiltration targets; restricted multimodal inference defaults on-node. When a catalog vision route is required, RMA mandates explicit labeling, packet provenance, optional sleeve, and never silent fallback.

Data Protection Stack

Product UIs often reduce “privacy” to a banner. RMA treats custody as a layered protocol with explicit failure modes. Let sensitivity $\lambda(B)\in\{\mathsf{public},\mathsf{internal},\mathsf{restricted}\}$.

A. L0 — Classification

\[ \rho(B)= \begin{cases} \mathsf{on\text{-}node}, & \lambda(B)=\mathsf{restricted}\\ \mathsf{prefer\text{-}node}, & \lambda(B)=\mathsf{internal}\\ \mathsf{any\text{-}labeled}, & \lambda(B)=\mathsf{public} \end{cases} \tag{5} \]

Under-labeling violates custody goal G3; over-labeling reduces utility.

B. L1 — Privacy Sleeves (Data in Motion)

Requester $u$ submits ciphertext task $\widetilde{T}$. Relay $\mathcal{R}$ strips transport identity before compute [14]. Private mode targets near-zero mutual information $I(U;L_m)\approx 0$ between requester identity and compute-side logs, acknowledging global passive adversaries remain out of scope.

C. L2 — Minimization and Handles

Packets store $h(m_j)=\mathrm{Hash}(m_j)$ and excerpts, not entire archives. Raw blobs remain in desk-controlled storage—separating auditability from retention of pixels.

D. L3 — Encryption and Retention

Desk artifacts SHOULD be encrypted under desk keys. Negative-evidence rows may outlive raw media when policy requires audit without keeping biometrics—a deliberate asymmetry.

E. L4 — Route Provenance

Every reply/packet records $r\in\{\mathsf{decentralized/on\text{-}node},\,\mathsf{centralized/catalog}\}$. Silent offload is a protocol violation even if the answer is “correct.” Honesty is part of security.

F. L5 — Fail-Closed

\[ \lambda(B)=\mathsf{restricted}\ \land\ \neg A_{\mathrm{private}}\ \Rightarrow\ \mathrm{Accept}(B)=\mathsf{false} \tag{6} \]

Fail-open assistants optimize engagement; fail-closed desks optimize non-exfiltration. Sleeves are not endpoint security; screenshots bypass packet controls; DP is not claimed for generative outputs [22]. The contribution is a coherent custody control plane aligned with high-stakes desk practice.

ProofPath Protocol

Atomize → Witness → Contradict → Negative evidence → Replay → Publish gate. Empty searches are first-class rows in $\mathcal{N}$ [17], not silence. If restricted and private capacity is unavailable, the algorithm refuses before inference (L5).

Publish Gate Formalism

Policy $\Pi=(\tau_\kappa,q_{\min},\mathcal{W}_{\mathrm{req}},\mathsf{strict})$ induces $\Gamma(P,\Pi)\in\{\mathsf{ready},\mathsf{review},\mathsf{blocked}\}$:

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathsf{blocked} &\Leftarrow \exists\,c_i:\ \kappa_i\ge\tau_\kappa\ \land\ \mathsf{strict}\\ \mathsf{ready} &\Leftarrow \forall\,c_i:\ \kappa_i<\tau_\kappa\ \land\ |\mathcal{W}_i|\ge q_{\min}\\ \mathsf{review} &\Leftarrow \text{otherwise} \end{aligned} \tag{7} \]

Export refuses $\mathsf{blocked}$ packets by default—editorial caution as a predicate [18].

Novelty and Positioning

Scientific novelty in systems work is often compositional: a new arrangement of known primitives that enables new guarantees. RMA’s claim is of that kind—not a new backbone, but a new control plane. We position it as eight numbered claims.

  1. N1 — Typed multimodal atoms. Unlike single-score chat and limited FEVER extractors [8], [9], RMA refracts bundles into $\tau(c_i)\in\{\mathsf{text},\mathsf{vision},\mathsf{doc},\mathsf{table},\mathsf{calc},\mathsf{cross}\}$.
  2. N2 — Deterministic calc veto. Unlike RAG/LLM stacks that hope a second model notices arithmetic errors [10], [1], $\mathrm{Calc}$ can increment $t_i$ via $\mathsf{mismatch}$.
  3. N3 — Orthogonal witness quora. Unlike corpus-only checking [8], publication requires multi-plane votes with measurable orthogonality $\Omega$ (Eq. 3).
  4. N4 — Negative evidence as schema. Unlike ephemeral UI traces, empty searches are first-class rows in $\mathcal{N}$ [17].
  5. N5 — Export-blocking gate $\Gamma$. Unlike soft scores, $\Gamma\in\{\mathsf{ready},\mathsf{review},\mathsf{blocked}\}$ can refuse export under $\kappa_i\ge\tau_\kappa$ [18].
  6. N6 — Durable cosigned packets. Unlike multi-agent debate that discards intermediate turns [6], [7], RMA seals $(G,\delta,\sigma)$ for replay.
  7. N7 — Structured epistemic notarization. Unlike ledger blob timestamps alone [19], cosigning covers claims, votes, absences, and gate—not only existence at time $t$.
  8. N8 — Fail-closed custody protocol. Unlike “private chat” product modes, L0–L5 encode classification, sleeves [14], handles, encryption, route honesty, and $\mathrm{Accept}(B)=\mathsf{false}$ when private capacity is missing.

Worked example (sketch)

Prose asserts a \$12M diversion in March 2025 with a budget PDF image. Atoms $c_1$–$c_4$ plus a $\mathsf{cross}/\mathsf{calc}$ link are formed. If the temporal plane contests March while calc matches \$12M, $\kappa_2$ rises and strict $\Gamma$ returns $\mathsf{blocked}$. An empty FOIA hit enters $\mathcal{N}$—absence as evidence [17].

Conclusion

We presented RMA as a research systems design for multimodal, decentralized, custody-aware claim verification. By refracting composites into typed atoms, measuring spectral conflict across orthogonal planes, protecting data through a fail-closed stack, and sealing publish decisions under Proof-of-Inference cosignatures, RMA offers high-stakes desks a path to use generative models without surrendering epistemic standards or case files to a single cloud.
