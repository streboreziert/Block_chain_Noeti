Product pitch

Noeti

We are not building another AI. We make AI run where you want — on your PC, phone, private rack, or cloud — with a map that shows the hardware, and a seal that proves what ran.

Hardware control · Where routing · Canvas · ProofPath

I · The problem

Today’s AI products hide the machine. Work disappears into one vendor cloud. You get speed, and lose custody. Privacy becomes a toggle. Routing is an afterthought.

Teams that publish under pressure need the opposite: choose the hardware, see the path, keep sensitive drafts local, and only then ask for a smarter model.

II · What we sell

A spatial control plane for compute. Canvas draws Where zones around work. Pair a computer in one command. Point a phone into the board. Run local models on the desk that owns the job — or escalate to cloud when you mean to.

Models are interchangeable. Placement is the product.

IV · How it works

Where

Every region on the board names a machine class: private, local, specific PC, phone, mesh, or cloud. The map travels with the workflow.

Connect

One terminal command links a PC. A link pairs a phone. Online devices appear in the picker — real routing, not a placeholder label.

Seal

When the work matters, Seal writes a ProofPath: what ran, where it ran, and what contested. Share the receipt — not just the chat transcript.
