Privacy

Architecture, not a brochure

Most investigative AI tools offer strong workflows on one centralized cloud. That means drafts, sources, and claim graphs usually sit under one operator’s legal and technical perimeter.

Noeti argues a different privacy: independence. Sensitive work can use private or customer-controlled compute. Verification is multi-node. Witnesses receive claim-scoped payloads — not the entire investigation by default.

Private routing

Source-bearing jobs stay on private pools or customer nodes. Public mesh is for non-sensitive checks — not raw tip dumps to random peers.

Claim-scoped disclosure

Multi-witness verification sees atoms of claims, not the whole binder. Less surface area for accidental leakage.

Separation of identity and compute

Relay design separates who asked from who computed. Hash-only proofs can show a check happened without storing tip text on a public ledger.

Honest hybrid

Where certifications require AWS or similar, use them. Where independence and source protection matter, do not make one vendor the only brain.
