About Noeti

Hardware control for AI · not another chatbot

Noeti is a workspace for placing intelligence on machines you choose. We care less about crowning a model, and more about where the work runs — your laptop, a paired phone, a private rack, or cloud when you ask for it.

Canvas is the map: draw a Where zone, connect a PC in one command, pull a phone into the board, then Run. Editor is the desk for code — allow a project into Second Brain, edit on your machine’s terms, ask the coding helper with a cached filesystem map. When the stakes are high, Seal a ProofPath so the trail — what ran, and on which hardware — can travel with the work.

We start with people who publish under pressure — journalism, legal, research, compliance, finance, NGOs — anyone who cannot afford to pour a sensitive binder into someone else’s default cloud.

Models will keep changing. Control of the machine should not.
